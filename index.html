How it works

Four steps, from upload to result.

Upload

You submit a short MP3 or WAV clip through the web interface.

Preprocess

The audio is converted to a standard format and length.

MFCC extraction

The clip is turned into Mel-Frequency Cepstral Coefficients that describe its spectral character.

Random Forest

A trained classifier votes AI-generated or human-composed, and the share of votes is shown as a percentage.

About the study

Web-Based AI-Generated Music Detection Using MFCC and Random Forest Among First-Year PUP Students

PUP-QCPolytechnic University of the Philippines, Quezon City Campus
BSITUndergraduate thesis, A.Y. 2025–2026
MFCC + RFAudio features classified by a Random Forest

This tool is part of a thesis at the Polytechnic University of the Philippines, Quezon City Campus. It studies whether a detection aid helps first-year students tell AI-generated music from human-composed music. The system gives decision support only; you make the final call.

Uploaded audio is processed for this study only and is not stored.PUP Quezon City · BSIT Thesis
